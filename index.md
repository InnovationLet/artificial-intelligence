InnovationLet Artificial Intelligence is the AI desk of the InnovationLet network. We cover the models people actually deploy, the research behind them, and the policy that decides who is allowed to ship what. No benchmark theatre, no press-release adjectives. Just what changed and what it costs to run.

## What we cover

- **Models**: frontier and open-weight releases, training methods, and the evaluation that exposes the difference.
- **Research**: papers worth reading, replicated results, and the claims that don't survive a second testing run.
- **Agents**: tool use, orchestration, and the reliability gap between a demo and production.
- **Policy**: regulation, licensing, and the safety rules that shape what gets built next.

## How we report

We follow primary sources first: papers, model cards, filings, and repositories. When we cite a claim, we link it. When a vendor won't say how a model was trained, we note the omission rather than fill it in. The desk publishes daily, with a longer brief each week for subscribers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does InnovationLet Artificial Intelligence cover?**
Models, research, agents, and policy. Every story is filed under one of those four areas.

**How often is it updated?**
The desk publishes daily during the week. Subscribe to the newsletter for a weekly round-up.
